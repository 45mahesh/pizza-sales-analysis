\# 📊 Business Insights



\## 1. Revenue Analysis

\- The business generates significant revenue from a limited set of popular pizzas.

\- High-performing products contribute a large share of total revenue.



\## 2. Customer Demand Patterns

\- Orders are concentrated during peak hours (evening time).

\- This indicates strong demand during specific time windows.



\## 3. Product Performance

\- Certain pizza categories consistently outperform others.

\- Top-selling pizzas can be prioritized for promotions and inventory.



\## 4. Order Behavior

\- Customers tend to order multiple items per transaction.

\- This contributes to increased average order value.



\## 5. Category Insights

\- Some categories generate more revenue due to pricing and demand.

\- Category-level analysis helps in strategic menu planning.



\---



\# 💡 Recommendations



\- Increase stock for high-demand pizzas

\- Optimize staffing during peak hours

\- Introduce offers for low-performing products

\- Focus marketing on best-selling categories
